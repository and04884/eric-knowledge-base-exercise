
# Hardware Troubleshooting
Troubleshooting hardware requires a systematic approach. Many issues stem from misconfigurations, failing components, or environmental factors.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting
### 1. Identify Symptoms
Slow performance, unexpected shutdowns, or device failures.  
### 2. Check Connections
Loose cables or improperly seated components cause many issues.  
### 3. Test Components
Swap RAM sticks, test storage drives, or try alternate peripherals.  
### 4. Monitor Temperatures
Overheating can damage the [[cpu-basics|CPU]] and other components.

## Tools for Troubleshooting
- Diagnostic software  
- Temperature monitoring tools  
- Spare components for testing  

## Related Pages
Troubleshooting often involves checking [[memory-and-ram|RAM issues]], verifying [[storage-devices|storage health]], and ensuring I/O devices are functioning correctly.

A structured approach helps you resolve issues quickly and prevent future failures.
