
# Storage Devices
Storage devices hold data long-term, even when the computer is powered off. They come in multiple forms, each with strengths and trade-offs.

## Major Types of Storage
### Hard Disk Drives (HDD)
Mechanical drives with spinning platters.  
- Affordable  
- Large capacity  
- Slower than SSDs  

### Solid State Drives (SSD)
Flash-based storage with no moving parts.  
- Fast read/write speeds  
- More durable  
- Higher cost per gigabyte  

### NVMe Drives
High-speed SSDs that connect directly to the motherboard.  
- Extremely fast  
- Ideal for gaming and professional workloads  

## Choosing the Right Storage
Your choice depends on performance needs, budget, and workload. Many systems use a combination of SSD (for speed) and HDD (for capacity).

## Related Hardware
Storage interacts constantly with [[memory-and-ram|RAM]] and the [[cpu-basics|CPU]]. It also plays a role in [[hardware-troubleshooting|diagnosing hardware issues]] when systems slow down.

> “Storage determines how quickly your system can access the data it relies on.”

Understanding storage types helps you build a balanced and responsive system.
